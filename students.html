Which Majors Should Submit

One-line test: if your field touches either AI or video/film, it's for you — one of the eight tracks fits your work.

Technical core

Computer Science, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Electronic Information, Automation, Data Science & Big Data, Cybersecurity, Robotics, Communications

Creative & media

Adjacent — you fit too

Your paper doesn't need 'AI video' in the title — a course project on recommendation, image generation, editing scripts, watermarking, or immersive interaction is already a paper seed.

Three Things You Might Ask

Can an undergraduate or Master's student really submit?

Yes. The Best Student Paper Award only requires the first author to be an enrolled undergraduate or Master's student — no PhD required.

I've never written an English paper — is my level enough?

You don't have to start from scratch. Whichever course project, competition work, or lab result you have can become a 4-page IEEE paper in the 7 steps below. Unsure which track? Pick the closest one — it won't hurt review.

Where can I actually use the certificate?

The award certificate carries the IEEE conference header, is downloadable, and goes straight into your postgraduate recommendation, Master's, or PhD application — the one line a reviewer can instantly verify.

Turn Your Project into a Paper (7 Steps)

  1. Pick a project you already have (coursework, competition, lab work, thesis ideas)
  2. Match it to one of the eight tracks
  3. Write it as a 4-page IEEE two-column paper (in English)
  4. Anonymize — remove names and affiliations for double-blind review
  5. Submit via EDAS before October 8
  6. Receive your acceptance notice October 15
  7. Present and collect your award in Hefei, November 13–15
