About AIVTA 2026

The International Conference on AI Video Technology and Applications (AIVTA 2026) brings together researchers, practitioners, and industry leaders to explore the latest advances in artificial intelligence applications for video and film production.

AIVTA 2026 will be held in Hefei, Anhui Province, China, from November 13-15, 2026.

The conference features keynote speeches, technical sessions, panel discussions, and networking opportunities across eight research tracks. See the Call for Papers & Submission page for the complete scope.
