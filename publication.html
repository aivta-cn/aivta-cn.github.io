Journal Recommendation

SCI/SSCI Journal Opportunities

Outstanding papers will be recommended for publication in prestigious SCI/SSCI indexed journals. The Program Committee will identify exceptional papers during the review process and invite authors to submit extended versions to partner journals.

Selection Criteria for Journal Recommendation

  • High originality and significant contribution to the field
  • Rigorous methodology and comprehensive evaluation
  • Excellent presentation quality
  • Strong potential for further research impact

Camera-Ready Requirements

Final Paper Submission

  • Address all reviewer comments and suggestions
  • Include author names and affiliations (no longer anonymous)
  • Ensure paper passes IEEE PDF eXpress check
  • Submit IEEE copyright form
  • At least one author must register for the conference
  • Paper must be presented at the conference

Important: Papers that are not presented at the conference will not be included in the IEEE proceedings or submitted for indexing.
