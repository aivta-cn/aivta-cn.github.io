How to Register

Step 1 — Fill in the registration form

Open the 问卷星 form and fill in your contact details, registration type, and invoice information. Authors should enter their EDAS paper ID; listeners do not need one.

Step 2 — Pay in CNY

Pay by Alipay, WeChat Pay, or bank transfer using the details shown at the end of the form. Add “AIVTA + your name” in the payment note, and upload a screenshot of the payment.

Step 3 — Receive confirmation and invoice

The organizing committee will confirm your registration within 2-5 working days after payment. Your invoice will be issued by whichever of the two entities received your payment, using the details you provide in the form.

Details of The Attending Registration

Join as Presenter

If you are only interested in giving a presentation at the conference without publishing your paper in the proceedings, choose “Presenter” in the registration form and provide the title and abstract of your presentation. For further information, please contact us at: adv@aivta.org

Join as Listener

No paper is required to attend AIVTA 2026. Choose “Listener” in the registration form and complete your payment before the listener registration deadline (November 15, 2026). The listener fee includes access to all technical sessions, conference lunches, a souvenir, and the conference proceedings.

Details of The Registration Fees

Registration fees include:

  • Conference proceedings
  • Lunches during the conference
  • Conference souvenirs
  • Access to all technical sessions

Registration fees do NOT include:

  • Accommodation
  • Visa application fee
  • Transportation fare

Group registrations

The group rate of 1200 CNY per person applies to groups of at least three registered attendees. Each person should complete the form separately and enter the same group name; the organizing committee will verify the group size before confirming the rate.

Important Notes

  • At least one author for each accepted final paper must pre-register by November 11, 2026.
  • Completed registrations will be acknowledged by the Organizing Committee within 2-5 workdays after receiving your payment.
  • Accepted authors who cannot travel to Hefei may apply to present online. Apply by email to adv@aivta.org before the author registration deadline, quoting your EDAS paper ID; an approved online presentation fulfils the presentation requirement and your paper stays eligible for awards.
  • If you cannot attend or present at all, please contact the Organizing Committee in advance. With prior approval your paper is still published in the proceedings, and we will post the print proceedings to you together with the payment receipt/Fapiao.
  • The registration fee covers one paper of up to 4 pages. Pages 5-8 are charged at 400 CNY per extra page.

Refunds Policy

If the participants request cancellation and refund due to personal reasons, the following refund policy applies:

Time Before Conference Refund Amount
60 days or more ahead of the conference 70% of payment refund
30-60 days ahead of the conference 50% of payment refund
Within 30 days ahead of the conference 30% of payment refund
After conference No refund

Cancellation and refund requests must be made formally by email to adv@aivta.org. Refunds are processed by the entity that received the payment.

The organizing committee reserves the right to change the dates and place of the conference due to force majeure. Losses thus incurred from the force majeure events shall not be liable and refunds policy shall not apply as well.
