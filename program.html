Detailed Programme — 14 November 2026 (Morning)

Venue: Hefei Binhu International Convention and Exhibition Center, Hall 2, Zone B (合肥滨湖国际会展中心 2 号展厅 B 区会议室)

How to find the room: enter the hall through Entrance South-1 of Hall 2 — the meeting area is immediately on your right.

Time Programme Speaker / Notes
08:00–09:00 Registration and poster setup Poster boards open from 08:00; all posters in place by 09:00
09:00–09:15 Opening Ceremony Welcome addresses
09:15–10:10 Keynote Speech Prof. Cuiqing Jiang, Hefei University of Technology (45 min talk + 10 min Q&A)
10:10–10:15 Changeover —
10:15–10:30 Online presentation Seung Pyo Kang, Korea National University of Arts (12 min + 3 min Q&A)
10:30–10:45 Online presentation Weihao Zhong, Guangdong University of Finance & Economics (12 min + 3 min Q&A)
10:45–11:00 Tea break and poster viewing Poster area
11:00–12:00 Panel Discussion Participants to be announced
12:00 Lunch —

The order and timing above may be adjusted slightly on the day depending on how the session runs; any change will be announced on site.
