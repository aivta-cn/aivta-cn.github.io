Camera-Ready Deadline: November 20, 2026

Your paper must pass IEEE PDF eXpress checking and be submitted before this date. Papers that do not pass will not be included in the IEEE Xplore submission.

Five Steps to Submit Your Final Paper

1

Prepare your manuscript

Format your paper with the official IEEE conference template (A4, two columns). Do not add page numbers, headers or footers; conference information will be inserted by the organizer.

2

Create a PDF eXpress account

Go to ieee-pdf-express.org and create an account using Conference ID 71243X and your email address. Remember the password you set.

3

Submit your file for checking

Upload your PDF (or your Word / LaTeX source) for PDF Checking. PDF eXpress emails you a report within minutes — pass or fail, with a list of errors if any.

4

Fix and re-check until it passes

The most common failures are unembedded fonts, low-resolution images and non-standard page size. Correct them and submit again. There is no limit on the number of attempts.

5

Submit the certified PDF to the organizer

Once your paper passes, download the IEEE Xplore-compliant PDF and email it to adv@aivta.org with your paper ID in the subject line. Do not send the version that did not pass.

eCF

Sign the IEEE eCF

IEEE will send each author an Electronic Copyright Form (eCF) by email. It can be signed only once — the title and author list lock at the moment of signing. Please complete it before November 20, 2026.

Formatting Requirements

Item Requirement
Page size A4
Template Official IEEE conference proceedings template, two columns
Page numbers Do not insert — added by the organizer
Fonts All fonts must be embedded (Type 1 or TrueType). PDF eXpress will reject otherwise.
Images 300 dpi or higher; colour images in RGB or CMYK
Copyright notice Added by the organizer on the first page; authors do not add it
File format PDF only, certified by PDF eXpress

The PDF eXpress site for AIVTA 2026 has been requested from IEEE. If 71243X is not accepted when you first try, wait one working day and try again, or email adv@aivta.org and we will confirm the activation date.
