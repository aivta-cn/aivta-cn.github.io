Paper Requirements

Format Requirements

  • Paper length: 6,000 - 8,000 words (including references)
  • Format: IEEE Conference format (two-column, 10pt font)
  • File type: PDF format only
  • Page limit: Maximum 8 pages (including figures and tables)

Language Requirements

  • Main text: English (required)
  • Abstract: English abstract required (150-250 words)
  • Chinese abstract: Required for all submissions (200-300 Chinese characters)
  • Keywords: 4-6 keywords in both English and Chinese

Content Requirements

  • Original work: Papers must present original, unpublished research
  • Not under review: Papers cannot be under review at other venues
  • Anonymization: Submissions must be anonymized for double-blind review
  • Ethical compliance: Research must comply with ethical guidelines

Review Process

Double-Blind Peer Review

All submissions will undergo a rigorous double-blind peer review process. Each paper will be reviewed by at least three members of the Program Committee.

Review Criteria

  • Originality and novelty of the research
  • Technical soundness and methodology
  • Significance and relevance to conference topics
  • Clarity and quality of presentation
  • Experimental evaluation (where applicable)

Anonymization Guidelines

  • Remove author names and affiliations from the paper
  • Remove self-citations or refer to them in third person
  • Remove acknowledgements that may reveal identity
  • Remove any identifying information from metadata

Submission Process

How to Submit

Submission Email: adv@aivta.org

Submission Steps

  1. Prepare your paper according to the IEEE conference format
  2. Ensure your paper is anonymized for double-blind review
  3. Convert your paper to PDF format
  4. Send your paper to adv@aivta.org with subject line: [AIVTA 2026 Submission] Paper Title
  5. Include author information in the email body (not in the paper)
  6. Wait for confirmation email within 3 working days

Email Body Should Include

  • Paper title
  • All author names and affiliations
  • Corresponding author contact information
  • Selected topic track (1-8)
